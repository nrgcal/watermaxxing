The Story

Reviving the Golden Era of Funk

Human Rhythm Records was born from a simple belief: the groove never died, it just went underground. We're bringing back the horn-driven arrangements, the analog warmth, and the unstoppable rhythm that defined a generation.

Every track in our catalog carries the DNA of the masters—but speaks to today. This isn't nostalgia. This is evolution. This is funk music doing what it's always done: making you move.
