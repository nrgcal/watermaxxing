INSTALLATION (DEVELOPER MODE)

1

Download & Unzip

Download the ZIP file above and extract it to a folder on your computer.

2

Open Extensions Page

In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions/ (just type it in the address bar)

3

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner of the page.

4

Load the Extension

Click "Load unpacked" and select the unzipped barron-live-extension folder.

5

Done!

Refresh any webpage. The ticker will appear at the top. Use the SLOW/FAST toggle to change speed, or click × to hide it for the session.
