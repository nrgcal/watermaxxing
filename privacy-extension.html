Privacy Policy

WATERMAXXING Chrome Extension

Effective Date: March 2026

Overview

The WATERMAXXING Chrome Extension ("the Extension") is a simple entertainment tool that displays a scrolling status ticker at the top of web pages. This privacy policy explains our data practices.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data.

The Extension:

  • Does NOT collect personal information
  • Does NOT collect browsing history
  • Does NOT track your activity
  • Does NOT transmit any data to external servers
  • Does NOT use cookies or analytics
  • Does NOT store any user data

How the Extension Works

The Extension injects a purely visual, decorative ticker bar at the top of web pages. All content displayed in the ticker is pre-written and bundled within the extension itself. The Extension operates entirely locally on your browser.

Permissions

The Extension requires "host permissions" solely to display the ticker on web pages. This permission is used only to add the visual ticker element to the page DOM. No data is read, collected, or transmitted.

Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this policy accordingly.
